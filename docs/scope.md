# Scope

## In scope

- public consortium structure
- public PI and lab lists
- public paper metadata
- public dataset/resource metadata
- derived evidence tables and pathway summaries
- reproducible scripts for collection and normalization

## Out of scope

- copyrighted full texts unless redistribution is clearly permitted
- restricted datasets
- private correspondence
- unpublished internal notes unless explicitly intended for public release

## Collection boundaries

Three paper collections are maintained separately:

- `current-program`: the official current Systems Biology for Infectious Diseases grant-query corpus
- `pi-backlog`: broader prior and newly published PI/lab papers
- `consortium-subset`: curated subset relevant to downstream cross-virus reasoning
